## 🚀 Overview
Converting video can sound technical, but HandBrake for Windows makes it genuinely simple. If you've ever wondered what HandBrake is, it's a free, open-source video converter that changes video into formats your devices can play — and it's friendly enough for first-timers. The secret is presets: instead of learning codecs and bitrates, you just pick a target like a phone, tablet, or general web use, and HandBrake applies sensible settings for you. Learning how to use HandBrake really is that quick: open a file, choose a preset, and start. When you're ready for more, the same app offers deeper controls, but you never have to touch them to get a good result. It's an approachable, no-cost way to convert and compress video on a Windows PC.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open HandBrake on your Windows PC.
2. Click **Open Source** and choose the video you want to convert.
3. Pick a preset that matches the device you'll play the video on.
4. Choose where to save the converted file.
5. Click **Start Encode** and let HandBrake do the rest.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is HandBrake free?**
Yes. HandBrake is free and open source, so you can download and use it without any cost.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
HandBrake runs on 64-bit Windows, primarily Windows 10 and Windows 11. A currently supported version is recommended.

**Is HandBrake safe to use?**
Yes. HandBrake is open source and transparent. For safety, download HandBrake from its official site and keep it updated to the latest version.

**Do I need to understand codecs to use HandBrake?**
No. HandBrake's presets handle the technical settings for you, so you can convert video just by choosing your target device and clicking start.

**Is my data secure?**
HandBrake runs locally on your own computer and uploads nothing. Your original and converted videos stay on your PC.
